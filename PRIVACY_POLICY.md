# Privacy Policy for Whack-A-Word-Ham
*Last updated: September 2026*

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game **Whack-A-Word-Ham** ("the Game"), created and distributed by the developer ("we", "us", or "our").

We believe that privacy is a fundamental right. Whack-A-Word-Ham is built as a fun, family-friendly offline word scramble puzzle game. We do not collect, harvest, sell, or share personal data.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
Whack-A-Word-Ham does **not** require user registration, accounts, or personal data to play.
- **Personal Information:** We do not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address).
- **Device & Location Data:** We do not collect GPS location data, device identifiers, IP addresses, contacts, photos, or browsing history.
- **Gameplay & Progress Data:** Game progress (such as unlocked stages, coin count, high scores, and unlocked hamster wardrobe accessories) is stored **locally on your device** in private application storage. This data never leaves your device.

---

### 2. Advertising & Third-Party Services
Whack-A-Word-Ham integrates **Google AdMob** (a mobile advertising service provided by Google LLC) to serve interstitial advertisements between stage completions. 

To deliver and personalize ads, measure ad performance, and combat fraudulent traffic, Google AdMob and Google Play Services may automatically collect and process certain device information, including:
- **Advertising Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (AAID) / pseudonymous identifier.
- **Device & Technical Data:** Device model, operating system version, carrier, language settings, and coarse network/IP connection data.
- **Ad Interaction & Diagnostic Data:** Ad impressions, clicks, crash logs, and performance metrics.

For more details on how Google collects and uses your data, please review:
- **Google Privacy Policy:** [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps that Use Our Services:** [https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- **Google AdMob Information:** [https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

You can manage or reset your advertising identifier and opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings under **Settings > Google > Ads**. We do not use any other third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

---

### 3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Our game is designed to be safe and suitable for players of all ages, including children under 13. In compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy laws:
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
- We do not track children across third-party websites or services.
- No personal data is requested or transmitted at any point during gameplay.

---

### 4. Data Retention & Deletion
Because all gameplay data (levels, coins, settings) is stored exclusively on your device, you have total control over your data:
- You can reset game progress at any time within the in-game settings.
- Uninstalling the app from your device permanently deletes all local gameplay data.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
